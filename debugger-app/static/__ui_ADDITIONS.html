Movement filmstrip small

A row of clickable movement chips above the timeline — name, color, duration, current one highlighted. One click jumps the playhead to that movement's start; pairs with the [ ] shortcuts. The colored timeline segments stay for proportion; the chips carry the names.

B

Graph crosshair readout small

Hovering a PID graph shows a crosshair + value tag: exact time and per-series voltage under the pointer (all series when breakdown is on). Click to snap the playhead there. Turns the graphs from pictures into instruments.

C

Ghost divergence readout small

While a ghost is pinned, a thin tether line connects the two robots and a chip reads the live gap: Δ position, Δ heading, and time lead/lag. Instantly answers "where do my two runs split?" — the moment the tether stretches, you've found it.

Run report card medium

One panel (or overlay) that grades the whole run: per movement — exit reason, final error, settle time, duration. Timeouts and fat errors glow red. This is the "which movement do I tune next" view, and it screenshots straight into the notebook.

E

Velocity graph medium

Linear + angular velocity derived from the 10 ms odometry deltas — zero firmware changes. Read against the voltage graphs above it: flat-topped v at max V = saturation, v sagging while V is pinned = wheel slip, and decel ramps show whether the governor actually bites.

F

Notebook PNG export small

An export button on the field panel: transparent PNG of the real run — path, waypoints, exit geometry, robot at the playhead — sized to match your diagram-app exports. Real telemetry drops into notebook pages beside the planned-path diagrams.
