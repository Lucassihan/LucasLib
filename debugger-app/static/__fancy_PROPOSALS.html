Fancy element proposals

Six candidates, mocked in the app's design language. Reply with numbers to build (e.g. "1, 2 and 6").
1

Comet playhead small

On the Drive/Turn graphs, a glowing dot rides each series line at the playhead, with a short comet trail fading behind it. Scrubbing feels physical, and the current value is readable right off the curve.

2

Progressive path reveal small

The path draws itself as the run plays: full brightness behind the robot, ~15% ahead of it, soft glow at the head. Where the robot has been vs where it's going is legible at a glance. Toggle in settings.

3

Velocity heat path medium

Path stroke width + color encode speed (computed from the 10 ms odometry deltas — no firmware change). Slow creep is thin and dim, full-tilt is thick and cyan. Braking zones and hesitations pop out instantly.

4

Stage-flip pulse small

The instant a movement changes stage during playback (boomerang FAR→MID→END, moveTo far→close), a ring ripples out from the robot on the field and the stage badge flashes. You see exactly where on the path each handoff happened.

Governor clamp glow small

Whenever a governor is actively limiting the output at the cursor (accel/decel ramp, min/max, slip cap), that bar's edge heats up red in the Control pipeline. Today the clamp is a number you have to notice; this makes it a state you can't miss.
